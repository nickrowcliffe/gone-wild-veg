About Gone Wild Veg

Growing with nature, close to home.

Gone Wild Veg is a not-for-profit community-interest market garden being developed on a four-acre site on the outskirts of Marlow.

The project

Good food, healthy soil and more wildlife.

Our aim is to grow seasonal vegetables, fruit, herbs and flowers using no-dig, regenerative methods without synthetic pesticides, herbicides or artificial fertilisers.

The productive areas will sit within a wider landscape designed to support biodiversity, including orchard trees, chalk wildflower grassland, rough meadow and other habitat areas.

As the project develops, we intend to supply local people through community-supported agriculture and markets, as well as local restaurants and independent retailers.

The site

A four-acre field on the edge of Marlow.

The site has a gentle south-facing slope, water supply, vehicle access and hard standing. Our plans include outdoor vegetable growing, flowers, orchard and food-forest planting, wildflower grassland and polytunnels.

The intention is to create a working market garden that is productive and commercially credible while also being a place where nature can thrive.

Meet the team

The people behind Gone Wild Veg

Dee is a former medical doctor. She holds a medical degree and an MSc in Nutritional Medicine and spent ten years in independent medical practice in nutritional and environmental medicine. Her interests include the environment, food, soil, the gut microbiome and health. She has five years' allotment experience and created the model vegetable and flower garden for the Compleat Angler Hotel in Marlow in 2023–24.

Nick Rowcliffe
